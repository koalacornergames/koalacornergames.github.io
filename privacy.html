Privacy Policy

Koala Cozy: Oddly Satisfying · Last updated 2 November 2026

Koala Cozy: Oddly Satisfying is a casual game for Android made by Ali Mansour. This page explains what data the game uses. You don't need an account to play, and we don't ask for your name, email address or location.

Data kept on your phone

Your progress, settings, best scores and whether you bought the unlock are saved on your phone. We don't receive them. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them, and Reset progress in Options erases your progress and best scores.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect data from your device, such as its advertising ID, IP address, approximate location from that address, device information and how you interact with ads. Google handles this data under its own policy; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it, such as in the EU and UK, Google's consent form asks for your choice before ads use your data, and you can change it under Ad privacy choices in Options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Purchases (Google Play)

The optional one-time purchase is handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The game only asks Google Play whether your account owns the purchase, so it can be restored on a new phone.

Vibration and sound

The game uses your phone's vibration and speaker for feedback. It doesn't use your microphone, camera, contacts or files.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, please contact us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as access or deletion. We don't hold any data that identifies you unless you email us. For data held by Google, use the links above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
